## Deviations from ROADMAP

1. **Exercise coverage**: the ROADMAP specifies "syscall counting, file-open tracing, block IO latency, scheduling latency, short-lived process capture, user-space uprobe" (6 topics). We added two additional exercises (signal delivery, syscall latency) for a total of 8, exceeding the minimum.
2. **Static linking**: trigger programs for exercises 1, 2, 5, and 8 use `-static` compilation. This was necessary because dynamically-linked binaries produce extra `openat` calls from `ld.so` (inflating counts) and bpftrace's `str()` fails to read filenames from some user-space addresses in dynamically-linked code. Documented in `docs/M1-design.md`.
3. **Binary name length**: the kernel's `comm` field is limited to 16 characters. Exercise 5's original binary name (`ex05_shortlived_proc`, 20 chars) was shortened to `ex05_slp` (8 chars).
